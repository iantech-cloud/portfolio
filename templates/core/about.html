the throughline

Curiosity is
the constant.

I am interested in understanding how things work, building things from scratch, solving difficult problems, and constantly learning new technologies.

I do not see technology as a set of isolated specialties. Mathematics helps me frame problems. Statistics helps me understand data. Programming turns ideas into systems. Cybersecurity helps me protect them, while business keeps the work connected to real value.

That combination is the way I approach every project: analytical, practical, and always learning.

background

From evidence
to action.

My academic background is rooted in mathematics and chemistry, with Statistics as my major area of study. I graduated with honors and developed a strong interest in analytical thinking, research, problem solving, and working with data.

Statistics taught me to break down a problem, understand the information available, identify patterns, test assumptions, and make decisions based on evidence. My interest in technology grew from there into programming, web development, networking, cybersecurity, data science, and system architecture.
